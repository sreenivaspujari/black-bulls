    FAQ

    Does MEDI-VISION diagnose disease?

    No. It's a decision-support prototype. It flags images that may need attention and helps order the review queue. A qualified clinician makes every diagnosis.

    Which body parts can I upload?

    Any of them: chest, head, spine, abdomen, pelvis, arms, legs and more, as X-ray, CT, MRI or ultrasound images. The quality and blur check works on all of them.

    Is the blur detection real?

    Yes. Sharpness is measured on your image using the variance of the Laplacian (a standard edge-sharpness measure), along with brightness and contrast checks. The thresholds are heuristics tuned for this demo.

    Is the heatmap from a trained model?

    Not in this prototype. Demo patients use prepared overlays. For uploads, the regions come from a simple brightness-anomaly heuristic that works on any grayscale image. Both are labelled "illustrative". A validated model (with Grad-CAM, for example) would replace this.

    What does "model confidence" mean?

    It's how strongly the output matches a trained pattern. It is not the probability that the patient has the disease, and it isn't diagnostic accuracy.
